1Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how information is handled in "RateLens" for iOS and Android (the "App"). The App is provided by an independent developer ("the developer," "we," "us").

The App reads prices on price tags in your camera view or photos and shows them converted into the currency you choose. There is no account registration. The developer does not operate any server and never asks you to enter your name, address, or phone number.

2Camera and Photos

  • Text in the camera view and in photos you choose is recognized entirely on your device using Google ML Kit text recognition. Camera images and photos are never sent to the developer or to any third-party server.
  • The App reads only the single photo you pick from your photo library. It does not read your whole library.
  • The camera and photos are used only after you allow access in the system permission prompt. You can withdraw permission at any time in your device settings.
  • According to Google, ML Kit may send metrics about the performance and use of its features (such as device information) to Google. These metrics do not include images or recognized text. See the ML Kit Terms.

3Information Stored Only on Your Device

  • Conversion history (a copy of the photo you took or chose, the amounts read, the currencies, and the date of the rate used). Up to 200 entries are kept; the oldest are deleted automatically.
  • The most recently downloaded exchange rates (used when you are offline)
  • Settings such as your chosen currency, and timestamps used to space out ads

This information is stored only in the App's private storage on your device and is never sent to the developer or to third parties. You can delete history entries one by one in the App. Uninstalling the App deletes all of it. Because we do not have this information, we cannot restore or delete it for you.

4Exchange Rates

To get exchange rates for conversion, the App connects to the public services below. The second is used only when the first cannot be reached.

  • Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) — about the service
  • currency-api (latest.currency-api.pages.dev) — about the service

The App sends only a request for the rates of certain currencies. It never sends photos, amounts, history, or information that identifies you. As with any internet connection, your IP address and basic connection data reach the operators of these services, and they handle it under their own policies.

5Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner ads, interstitial (full-screen) ads, and rewarded ads (ads you may choose to watch to see all of your history for a limited time) through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve ads, the following information is sent from your device to Google and handled by Google:

  • Identifiers such as advertising IDs (on Android, the advertising ID and App Set ID)
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate your approximate location)
  • Device and app information (model, operating system and version, language, app version, etc.)
  • Ad interactions such as impressions, taps, and video views
  • Diagnostic information about the SDK

The App does not ask for tracking permission through iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT), so the IDFA is not used on iOS.

To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, Advertising at Google, and the Google Privacy Policy.

6Ad Consent (EEA, UK, etc.)

In regions where the law requires consent, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK) to ask for your consent to the use of information for advertising when you open the App, in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy.

  • You can use the App without consenting. In that case, Google shows non-personalized or limited ads.
  • Your consent status is stored on your device. If you uninstall and reinstall the App, you will be asked again.
  • If you live in the EEA or a similar region, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and withdrawal of consent. Because the developer does not hold advertising data, you can also exercise your rights for data held by Google through the Google Privacy Policy.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on many devices, Settings → Google → Ads). Menu names vary by device.

7Purchases (RateLens Pro)

We plan to offer a paid plan, "RateLens Pro" (a one-time purchase or an annual auto-renewing subscription). If we do, payments will be processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The developer never receives your credit card or other payment details. Store purchase records are used to confirm your purchase status.

8Information You Give Us When You Contact Us

If you email us, we use your email address, your name (if included), and the content of your message to reply to you. We keep it only as long as needed to reply, then delete it.

9Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, address, or phone number (unless you include them in a message to us)
  • Contacts, microphone, or precise (GPS) location
  • Data from analytics or crash-reporting tools (the App currently uses none; we will update this Policy if that changes)

We do not sell your personal information for money.

10Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11International Processing

Because we use services such as Google, information may be processed in data centers outside Japan, including in the United States.

12Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when laws change or when we add features (such as starting to offer the paid plan). When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date on this page.
