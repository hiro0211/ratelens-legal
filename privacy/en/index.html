1Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how information is handled in the app "RateLenz" (the "App"). The App is provided by DefactoTech (Hiroaki Arimura) ("the developer," "we," "us").

The App reads prices on price tags in your camera view and shows them converted into the currency you choose. There is no account registration or sign-in. The developer does not operate its own server and never asks you to enter your name, address, or phone number.

The App uses Google AdMob to serve ads and RevenueCat to manage purchases of the paid plan, and Google's Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used. Sections 5 to 8 explain what information is sent to these services.

2Camera and Photos

  • Text on price tags is recognized on your device. On iOS, the App uses Apple's Vision framework. The Android version uses Google ML Kit text recognition on the device.
  • Camera images, photos, and the recognized text and amounts are never sent to the developer or to any third-party server. In the Android version, which uses ML Kit, images are only read on the device and are not sent to Google.
  • However, the ML Kit library included in the Android version itself sends the following information to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics: device information (manufacturer, model, and OS version), app information (package name and version), per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, configuration, and error codes. According to Google, this information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred to third parties. For details, see ML Kit's data disclosure.
  • When you convert from a photo, the App reads only the single photo you pick. It does not read your whole library.
  • On iOS, you pick one photo from your photo library. The camera and photos are used only after you allow access in the system permission prompt.
  • On Android, the App uses the system photo picker, and only the one photo you pick is passed to the App. The App does not request photo permissions on Android. The camera is used only after you allow access in the system permission prompt.
  • You can withdraw camera and other permissions at any time in your device settings.

3Information Stored Only on Your Device

  • Conversion history: a copy of the photo you took, the amounts read, the currencies, and the date of the rate used. Up to 200 entries are kept, and the oldest are deleted automatically.
  • The most recently downloaded exchange rates, used when you are offline.
  • Settings such as your chosen currency, the number of scans you made that day, and timestamps used to space out ads.
  • The time you first opened the App, and a cached record of whether you have the paid plan.

This information is stored only in the App's private storage on your device and is never sent to the developer or to third parties. You can delete history entries one by one in the App.

Backups and deletion work differently on Android and iPhone:

  • Android: The App excludes this information from Google's backup. It is also not carried over when you transfer data to a new device. Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.
  • iPhone: Depending on your device settings, history and settings may be included in iCloud Backup or in a backup on your computer. iCloud Backup is stored by Apple, and the developer cannot see it. If you do not want this information kept in a backup, turn off backup for the App in your device settings or delete the backup.
  • iPhone: The number of scans that day, the ad-spacing timestamps, the time you first opened the App, and the cached purchase status are stored in the iPhone keychain, so that deleting and reinstalling the App does not reset your scan count. Keychain information may remain on your device after you uninstall the App, and may also be included in backups.

The developer does not have the on-device information described in this section, so we cannot restore or delete it for you. To delete purchase information (Section 7), see Section 13.

4Exchange Rates

The App downloads the exchange rates used for conversion from the public services below. The second is used only when the first cannot be reached.

  • Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) — about the service
  • currency-api (latest.currency-api.pages.dev) — about the service

The App sends only a request for the rates of certain currencies. It never sends photos, amounts, history, or information that identifies you. As with any internet connection, your IP address and basic connection data reach the operators of these services, and they handle it under their own policies.

5Advertising (Google AdMob) and Tracking

The App shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK: interstitial (full-screen) ads, and rewarded ads that you may choose to watch to get more scans for the day. To serve ads, your device sends the following information to Google, and Google handles it:

  • Advertising identifiers. On iOS, the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) is sent only if you allow tracking. On Android, the advertising ID and App Set ID are sent. If you delete your advertising ID in your device settings, the App no longer sends it. See the end of Section 6 for how to delete or reset it.
  • IP address, which Google may use to estimate your approximate location. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Advertising data and interactions, such as impressions, taps, and video views.
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system and version, language, and app version.
  • Diagnostic data about the SDK's performance, crashes, and errors.

On iOS, the App asks for permission to track you through App Tracking Transparency (ATT). If you allow it, Google may use the IDFA to show ads tailored to you, combined with your activity in other companies' apps and websites. If you do not allow it, you can still use every feature of the App, and the IDFA is not sent. You can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

While you have an active paid plan, the App does not show ads.

If you see an inappropriate ad, please tell us through Settings → Report an Inappropriate Ad in the App, or by email at the address below.

To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, Advertising at Google, and the Google Privacy Policy.

6Ad Consent (EEA, UK, etc.)

In regions where the law requires consent, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK). When you first use the App, it asks for your consent to the use of information for advertising, in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy.

  • You can use the App without consenting. In that case, Google shows non-personalized or limited ads.
  • If you do not consent, the App does not show the ATT prompt and does not send the IDFA.
  • Your consent status is stored on your device. In these regions, you can change your choices from the App's settings screen.
  • If you live in the EEA or a similar region, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and withdrawal of consent. The developer does not hold advertising data. For data held by Google, you can also use the procedures in the Google Privacy Policy.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on many devices, Settings → Google → Ads). Menu names vary by device.

  • Resetting gives you a new advertising ID that is not linked to the previous one.
  • Deleting replaces your advertising ID with a string of zeros, and the App no longer sends an advertising ID.
  • On Android, Google's Privacy Sandbox features (such as ad topics) may be used for ads. You can turn them off under "Ad privacy" in your device settings.

7Paid Plan (RateLenz Premium) and RevenueCat

The App offers a paid plan, "RateLenz Premium." Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The developer never receives your credit card or other payment details.

To confirm your purchase status, the App uses the RevenueCat SDK provided by RevenueCat, Inc. The following information is sent to RevenueCat:

  • Purchase history, such as the plan you bought, purchase and renewal dates, and store transaction information.
  • An anonymous App User ID that the App creates automatically for each device. It is not linked to your name or email address.
  • Device and app information, such as operating system version, app version, country or region, and language.

The developer uses this information to confirm and restore purchases and to total sales. It is not used for advertising or tracking. To learn how RevenueCat handles information, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Purchase information is retained as follows:

  • The developer keeps purchase information in RevenueCat without a fixed end date, so that purchases can be restored and subscription status can be checked. When you ask us to delete it, we delete it as described in Section 13.
  • According to RevenueCat's documentation, when the developer deletes a customer, all of that customer's data, including purchase history, is cleared. RevenueCat processes the deletion asynchronously.
  • How long RevenueCat keeps information in backups or elsewhere follows RevenueCat's own policies. RevenueCat's Privacy Policy states that it retains personal data only as necessary to comply with its legal obligations.

8Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

To improve the App, the developer uses Google's Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase). We use it to learn which features are used and how often, where people stop using the App, and whether people keep using it. The App sends the following information to Google:

  • App instance ID. Firebase creates this number automatically for each installation. It is not linked to your name or email address. The developer does not set a user ID.
  • Actions in the App. For example: screens you open, progress through onboarding, how many times you scan and how many price tags are read, manual entry, currency changes, actions in history and settings, and actions and results on ads and the paid-plan screen. Counts and durations are sent as ranges such as "4 or more" or "5–15 seconds."
  • App state. For example: the currency pair you chose, light or dark appearance, the type of paid plan, and the results of the tracking and ad-consent prompts.
  • Device and app information. For example: device model, operating system and version, language, app version, when you first opened the App, and session length.
  • Approximate region. Google estimates your country or region from your IP address. Precise (GPS) location is not used.
  • Purchase events. Google automatically records the type and price of paid-plan purchases.
  • Advertising identifiers. On iOS, the IDFA may be sent only if you allow tracking. On Android, the advertising ID may be sent. If you delete your advertising ID in your device settings, it is no longer sent (Section 6).

The App does not send camera images, photos, recognized text and amounts, or the contents of your conversion history.

The developer uses this information only to improve the App and to measure how it is used. We do not use it for ad personalization or tracking. We do not use Google signals or link the data to Google Ads. The developer exports this information to Google BigQuery (a Google Cloud database) for analysis. Only the developer can access the BigQuery data, and we do not share it with third parties.

Consent works as follows:

  • In regions where consent is required, such as the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the App follows the choices you make in the consent form described in Section 6. If you do not consent, Google does not store identifiers and uses the information only for aggregate modeling that is not linked to you. Before the consent form appears, the App does not send this information.
  • You can change your choices from "Privacy choices" in the App's settings.
  • On iOS, if you do not allow tracking, the IDFA is not sent.

Retention: detailed data in the Google Analytics interface is deleted according to the Google Analytics data-retention setting. Data exported to BigQuery is kept without a fixed end date, so that we can compare long-term usage. Because the data is not linked to your name or email address, the developer cannot find a specific person's data. For deletion, see Section 13.

For how Google handles information, see Google Analytics data protection, Privacy and Security in Firebase, and the Google Privacy Policy.

9Information You Give Us When You Contact Us

If you email us, we use your email address, your name (if included), and the content of your message to reply to you. We keep it only as long as needed to reply, then delete it.

10Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, address, or phone number (unless you include them in a message to us)
  • Contacts, microphone, or precise (GPS) location
  • Camera images, photos, recognized text and amounts, or conversion history
  • Data from crash-reporting tools (the App currently uses none; we will update this Policy if that changes). For usage analytics, the App uses only Firebase Analytics, described in Section 8.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

11Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12Encryption in Transit and International Processing

All of the App's network communication is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS). This covers communication with the exchange-rate services, Google (AdMob, ML Kit, and Firebase Analytics), and RevenueCat.

Because the App uses services from Google and RevenueCat, information may be processed in data centers outside Japan, including in the United States.

13Deleting Your Data

The App has no accounts. You can delete each type of information as follows:

  • Information on your device (conversion history, exchange rates, and settings). You can delete history entries one by one in the App. On Android, uninstalling the App deletes all of it. On iPhone, the scan count and other information stored in the keychain may remain on the device. If information remains in an iPhone backup, please delete that backup as well (Section 3).
  • Purchase information (RevenueCat). Email us at the address below to request deletion, and the developer will delete the purchase information recorded in RevenueCat.
  • Advertising information (Google). The developer does not hold advertising data. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Section 6). For data held by Google, you can request deletion through Google's procedures.
  • Usage analytics information (Firebase Analytics). This information is not linked to your name or email address, so the developer cannot find a specific person's information. If you uninstall the App, the app instance ID on your device is deleted, and later information is no longer linked to it. In the EEA and similar regions, you can withdraw consent from "Privacy choices" in the App's settings (Section 8).

When you request deletion of purchase information, please include one of the following in your email so that we can find your purchase:

  • Android: your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA."). You can find it in the purchase confirmation email from Google Play.
  • iPhone: the order ID on your App Store receipt. You can find it in the receipt email from Apple.

We aim to reply within 14 days of receiving your request. Deleting purchase information does not cancel a subscription; please cancel through your store (Terms of Use, Section 5). If you use "Restore Purchases" after deletion, your purchase information will be recorded in RevenueCat again.
